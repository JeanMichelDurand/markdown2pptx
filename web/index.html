markdown2pptx

Turn a Markdown file into a PowerPoint deck on your own template: headings become chapters and slides, ```mermaid blocks native, editable PowerPoint shapes, tables native tables, lists real bullets. Runs entirely in your browser: nothing to install, and your text never leaves your computer.

Deck outline

  1. Write or open Markdown to see its slides.
Headings make the chapters and slide titles, a --- line starts a new slide, ::: notes blocks are the speaker notes. All the conventions. The first conversion loads Python in the page (about 15 MB, cached afterwards). Diagrams are drawn by mermaid2pptx: flowcharts, sequence diagrams and Gantt charts.
